How it works

Rental terms

  1. Documents

    Bring an ID card or passport. The rental agreement is signed when you pick up the gear.

  2. Deposit

    A refundable deposit of 20 000 ₸ per order is returned in full when the gear comes back.

  3. Pick-up & return

    Collect on the issue date after 10:00, return on the return date before 20:00. Same-day returns count as one day.

  4. Cleaning & damage

    Cleaning is included. Torn fabric or broken poles are charged at repair cost, never more than the deposit.

EMERCOM safety rules

  • Register your group and route with the rescue service of the Ministry of Emergency Situations before going into the mountains.
  • Check the weather and avalanche warnings on the day you leave.
  • Never hike alone; tell someone your return time.
  • Emergency: call 112.
